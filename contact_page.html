Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping into the arena.

casino

Do you charge extra for board games?

Absolutely zero! All 8 classic board games (Chess, Business, Uno, Ludo, Snake & Ladder, Stacking Tower, XO, and Cube) are 100% free to play with any food or drink purchase.

celebration

Can we host birthday parties or tournaments?

Yes! We routinely host squad birthdays, college team-ups, and private gaming tourneys. Reach out 24 hours prior to reserve our dedicated lounge section with custom combo platters.

local_parking

Are there parking facilities available?

Yes! Convenient two-wheeler and four-wheeler street parking is available directly along Link Road right outside the cafe entrance with good lighting and clear street access.

delivery_dining

Do you take delivery orders directly?

You can order directly online through our official Swiggy store link with instant doorstep delivery across Virudhunagar, or call our direct hotline for bulk takeaway orders.
