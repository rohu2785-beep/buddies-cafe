history_edu THE GENESIS OF BUDDIES

More Than Just Coffee.
A Sanctuary For Real Connections.

Buddies Cafe was born out of a simple, rebellious belief: waiting for food shouldn't mean staring down at glowing smartphone screens, and hanging out with your friends deserves more than sterile fast-food counters.

We envisioned a cozy, dimly lit nocturnal hideout right in Anna Nagar, Virudhunagar, where the smell of freshly toasted brioche buns and spicy chicken marinades mixes with the click-clack of dice rolls and victory cheers over Catan tiles.

Whether you are a college squad looking to celebrate after exams, a couple escaping routine for an artisanal cold brew, or a competitive group of tabletop tacticians, Buddies was built from the ground up for you.
